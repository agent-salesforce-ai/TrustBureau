The report

Anatomy of a TrustBureau file.

Six evidence classes, pulled fresh on every request. Not a stale database row — an investigation, run by AI that reads the primary record and cites it.

01

Identity & registration

Legal name resolution, DBA mapping, Secretary of State standing across all 50 states, officers, registered agents, incorporation history.

02

Public-record risk

UCC-1 positions and terminations, tax liens, judgments, bankruptcies, court dockets — the stacking picture competitors miss.

03

Contributed tradelines

Payment performance, defaults, and slow-pays reported by the funders who actually hold the paper. Give-to-get access.

04

Verified cash flow

Bank-statement intelligence: true revenue, balances, NSF days, existing debt service — with document forensics that flag tampered PDFs.

05

Digital footprint

Web presence, domain age, review velocity, licensing, adverse media. The open-web signals that catch shells and resurrections early.

06

TrustScore

One 0–100 score synthesized across every exhibit — delivered with reason codes, so an underwriter can defend the decision it informs.
