The file

Six classes of evidence, pulled fresh.

Not a stale database row — an investigation run at request time, against primary sources, with every finding cited.

EXHIBIT 01

Identity & registration

Legal name resolution, DBA mapping, Secretary of State standing, officers, registered agents and incorporation history.

EXHIBIT 02

Public-record risk

UCC-1 positions and terminations, tax liens, judgments, bankruptcies and federal dockets — the stacking picture others miss.

EXHIBIT 03

Contributed tradelines

Payment performance, defaults and slow-pays reported by the funders who actually hold the paper. Give-to-get access.

EXHIBIT 04

Verified cash flow

Bank-statement intelligence — true revenue, balances, NSF days, existing debt service — with forensics that flag tampered documents.

EXHIBIT 05

Digital footprint

Verified website and domain tenure, review standing, licensing and adverse media. The signals that catch shells and resurrections.

EXHIBIT 06

TrustScore

One 0–100 score computed from the exhibits by a fixed rubric, with reason codes on every point.

TrustScore

A score you can defend, not a black box.

The AI reads the record and classifies what it finds. The score itself is computed in code from a published rubric — so the same evidence always produces the same number, and every point traces to a source.
